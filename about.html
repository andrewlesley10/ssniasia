Who we are

A Sri Lankan Travel Company, Based in Colombo

SSNI Asia (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan private limited company incorporated on 17 February 2020 under the Companies Act No. 7 of 2007, bearing Registration Number PV 00220462.

Based in Colombo, SSNI Asia focuses primarily on inbound tourism, welcoming international travellers who wish to discover Sri Lanka's culture, natural beauty, historical heritage and hospitality. Alongside this, we also cater to outbound travel requirements.

Our aim is to offer carefully planned holidays that suit the interests, budgets and travel preferences of individuals, families, couples and groups.

The story behind the name

Four Initials, One Shared Vision

SSNIASIA

The name SSNI was created from the initials associated with the four directors who established the company. It represents their shared vision of building a travel and tourism business under one corporate identity.

The addition of Asia reflects the company's wider outlook and its ambition to connect travellers with destinations and experiences across the region. The company operates under the name SSNI Asia, with its online identity represented by www.ssniasia.com.

Our vision

Where We're Headed

To become a trusted Sri Lankan travel company recognised for personalised service, dependable travel arrangements and memorable holiday experiences.

Our mission

How We'll Get There

To help travellers explore Sri Lanka through well-organised, enjoyable and affordable travel experiences, while respecting local culture, communities and the natural environment.
