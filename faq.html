Good to Know Before You Go

Can't find your answer here? Call us on +94 11 478 9644 or send an enquiry.

What type of tourism does SSNI Asia specialise in?

We primarily focus on inbound tourism, helping international visitors plan holidays and sightseeing experiences in Sri Lanka. We also cater to outbound travel requirements.

Can I arrange a customised Sri Lanka tour?

Yes. You can request an itinerary based on your preferred destinations, travel dates, group size, interests and budget.

Do you arrange airport transfers?

Airport transfers can be included in your requested travel arrangements.

Can you arrange hotels and accommodation?

Hotel and accommodation arrangements can be included in customised holiday quotations, subject to availability.

Do you arrange family and group tours?

Yes. Our proposed services cover individual travellers, couples, families and groups.

How many days should I spend in Sri Lanka?

A seven-day holiday can cover several main attractions. Ten to fourteen days allows more time to combine historical destinations, wildlife, hill country and beaches.

When is the best time to visit Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka can be visited throughout the year. Weather conditions vary by region, so the most suitable itinerary depends on your travel dates and preferred destinations.

Do I need a visa to visit Sri Lanka?

Entry requirements depend on nationality and current immigration rules. Travellers should check official Sri Lankan immigration guidance before departure.

How do I request a quotation?

You can contact SSNI Asia by telephone or submit an enquiry through the website.
