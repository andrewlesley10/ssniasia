Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how S S N I ASIA (PVT) LTD (“SSNI Asia”, “we”, “us”), Company Registration No. PV 00220462, handles the personal information you share with us through www.ssniasia.com.

Information we collect

When you send a tour enquiry we receive the details you enter: your name, email address and message, and — if you choose to give them — your phone or WhatsApp number and country of residence.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also record basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare an itinerary or quotation.
  • To make the travel arrangements you ask us to make, such as hotel reservations, transfers and excursions.
  • To protect the website from misuse, for example by limiting repeated automated submissions.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Sharing

If you go ahead with a booking, we share only the details needed with the hotels, transport providers and other suppliers involved in your trip.

Retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting booking, and as required for our business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@ssniasia.com.

Third-party services

Our contact page shows an embedded Google Map and our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. These services may receive your IP address when the page loads, under Google's own privacy terms.
