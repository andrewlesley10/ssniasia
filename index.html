Welcome to SSNI Asia

Your Gateway to Sri Lanka

From the ancient kingdoms of Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa to the spectacular Sigiriya Rock Fortress, the misty mountains of Ella, the tea plantations of Nuwara Eliya and the golden beaches of the southern coast, Sri Lanka offers something for every traveller.

Whether you are planning a family holiday, honeymoon, cultural journey, wildlife safari, beach escape or group tour, SSNI Asia aims to help you discover the island at your own pace.
